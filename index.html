The Landline

The Landline is an old black phone that makes one call only: to you, at seven years old.

We carry it into streets, parks, offices and festivals. Strangers pick up the receiver. We ask one question. What happens next is between them and the kid on the other end of the line.

They picked up. What do you say?

Line 01Tell them one thing you know now that they needed then.

Line 02They ask if you are okay. What is the true answer?

Line 03What do you wish someone had asked you, when you were this small?

Almost every adult is walking around with a seven-year-old inside them who was fed, schooled and managed, but rarely asked a single question about who they were.

We think every childhood deserves to be heard. Starting with yours.

This is not therapy. It is not a brand campaign. It is a public act of listening, one receiver at a time.

Someone in your life
is seven right now.

Whatever you needed to hear back then, someone small needs to hear from you today. That is the whole point of picking up.
