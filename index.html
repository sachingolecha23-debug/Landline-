Why this matters

The first seven years shape all the rest.

How we were held. Whether we felt safe. Whether anyone wanted to know what we thought. It settles in early, and it stays.

Most of us were fed, taught and kept safe.

Fewer of us were ever really asked who we were. So we grew up, and quietly carried what went unheard.
